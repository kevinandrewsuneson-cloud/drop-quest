💧 How to Play Drop Quest

  1. Choose your role — Engineer, Diplomat, Scientist, or Logistics. Each has unique abilities.
  2. Pick a community on the world map. Red communities are in crisis — prioritise them!
  3. Complete 3 challenges per mission:
    🔩 Pipe Puzzle — rotate pieces to connect water flow
    ⚖️ Decision — choose how to handle political/social issues
    🏗️ Builder — design a water distribution system
  4. Earn points and unlock new communities. Collaborate with teammates for bonus multipliers.
  5. Track your impact — see how many people gained access to clean water.
